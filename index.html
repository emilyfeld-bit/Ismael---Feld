02 / OUR STORY

Designed with intention.

Ismael & Feld is built around a simple idea: style should feel personal, enduring and effortless. Each piece begins with a strong silhouette and is finished with details that reward a closer look.

Our visual language pairs deep black, warm ivory and muted metallic tones with typography inspired by classic fashion houses and modern editorial design.
